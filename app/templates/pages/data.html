A CLOSER LOOK AT THE DATA

From Documents to Insights

Explore how MongoDB documents support the EcoCraft marketplace and reveal patterns in its catalog.

Five collections

Users, products, carts, orders and reviews each have a place in the database.

Connected documents

Products reference their artists, carts embed item quantities, and orders keep product snapshots. Reviews connect customers, products and orders.

Category insights

A MongoDB aggregation groups approved, listed products by category to calculate product counts and average prices.

From cart to order

Checkout rechecks availability, updates inventory and creates a demo order using a MongoDB transaction. It does not process payment.
