CONNECTED DOCUMENTS

Embed what belongs together.
Reference what lives on its own.

EMBEDDED

A useful snapshot, close at hand.

Cart items keep a product reference and quantity together. Order items also retain product details and price information as a snapshot, so the record describes the purchase as it was made.

order.items { productSnapshot, quantity, unitPrice, shipping }
REFERENCED

A connection between separate records.

Products reference their artist. Carts and orders reference their customer, while reviews connect the customer, product and order they belong to.

review customerId → productId → orderId

CREATING, READING, UPDATING, DELETING

Database operations, made part of the experience.

Validation and role permissions shape which actions are available to each person.

C

Create

Register an account, submit a listing or leave an eligible review.

R

Read

Explore products, retrieve orders and view marketplace information.

U

Update

Edit eligible listings, adjust a cart or update shipping progress.

D

Delete

Remove cart items and other records where the user is permitted.

FROM CART TO ORDER

A purchase is a coordinated sequence.

Checkout rechecks product availability and artist eligibility, then updates inventory, creates the order and clears the cart inside a MongoDB transaction. If a transaction step fails, the changes are rolled back together.

FROM INFORMATION TO INTELLIGENCE

Different questions.
Different views of the data.

01 / CATALOG

Understanding the marketplace

A MongoDB aggregation groups listed, in-stock products by category and calculates product counts and average prices.

02 / ARTIST TOOLS

Empowering the artists

Artist insights summarize order activity, gross sales, commission, net payout and top products across a selected date range. Cancelled items are excluded from sales and payout totals.

03 / MARKETPULSE

Marketplace intelligence

MarketPulse presents a centralized view of customer engagement, artist performance, sales trends and product activity.

BUILDING TRUST THROUGH DATA

Reliable experiences start with careful handling.

EcoCraft combines application-level validation, role-based access and controlled database operations to help protect marketplace information and keep interactions consistent.

Project documentation also describes Atlas-side validators and indexes, configured manually, as additional data constraints.
