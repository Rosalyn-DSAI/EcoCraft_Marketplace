About EcoCraft

Thoughtfully made.
Meaningfully connected.

Bringing creativity, craftsmanship and conscious living together.

A small idea, made tangible

EcoCraft began with a simple idea: creating a space where craftsmanship meets conscious living. A marketplace where artists can share their creativity, customers can discover thoughtfully made products, and everyday purchases can become more meaningful.

Developed as part of our NoSQL database course, EcoCraft brings this vision to life through technology, creativity and a shared commitment to learning.

01 MADE WITH PURPOSE

Every handmade creation carries a story.

Behind every handmade creation is a story of creativity, care and craftsmanship. EcoCraft celebrates these stories by giving artists a space to showcase their work, from small-batch creations to products made with recycled, upcycled and locally sourced materials.

Our marketplace encourages discovery and more thoughtful purchasing decisions.

A place for good work to find its people.

SUSTAINABILITY IN EVERY DETAIL

More context for more considered discovery.

Artists can describe the materials and sustainability practices connected to their products. These details help customers find work that aligns with their preferences—from handmade small-batch production to upcycled materials and zero-waste packaging.

Practice details are artist-provided; EcoCraft does not independently verify every sustainability claim.

  • Recycled materials
  • Upcycled
  • Locally sourced
  • Zero-waste packaging
  • Handmade, small batch
  • Organic
  • Non-toxic finishes

BUILT FOR LEARNING

Classroom knowledge,
put into practice.

EcoCraft is a practical exploration of how database technology can support real application workflows. Through document modeling, application development, data validation and analytics, our team transformed classroom knowledge into an interactive marketplace experience.

A note on the demo

EcoCraft is an academic demonstration. Checkout simulates marketplace transactions and does not process real payments.
