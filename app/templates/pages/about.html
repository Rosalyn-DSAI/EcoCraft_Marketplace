OUR COURSE PROJECT

About EcoCraft

A marketplace for thoughtful finds, built to explore how a NoSQL database supports a real application.

Made with purpose

EcoCraft connects customers with handmade and sustainably made products while giving artists a place to share their creations.

More than a catalog

Customers browse, manage a cart and review purchases. Artists manage listings and shipping, and administrators review artists and products.

Built for learning

This class project demonstrates a working marketplace backed by MongoDB. Checkout is a demonstration and does not process payments.
