A COLLABORATIVE JOURNEY

Every meaningful creation begins with people. EcoCraft is the result of a collaborative journey that brought together different perspectives, skills and ideas to transform a classroom concept into a working digital marketplace.

Through research, design, development, testing and continuous improvement, our team explored what it means to build a connected application from the ground up.

A SHARED PRACTICE

01

Design & experience

Turning ideas into interfaces people can understand and use: shaping the visual identity, navigation, product discovery and experiences for different marketplace users.

02

Application & data

Building the connections behind the experience: designing MongoDB documents, developing application workflows and linking the interface to the information that powers it.

03

Testing & refinement

Bringing components together, examining how they interact and improving the experience through testing, troubleshooting and collaborative problem-solving.

These are areas of work, not a one-to-one list of team members.
